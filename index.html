Paddy Field Segmentation from Pléiades Satellite Imagery

Property Price Prediction from Listing Descriptions
DeBERTa-v3-large, ModernBERT-large, Hugging Face Transformers, Modal

Fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3-large and ModernBERT-large regressors to predict property prices from free-text listing descriptions across 14,640 training listings, combining multi-seed 5-fold runs through a log-space blend with frozen out-of-fold recalibration. Cross-Model Residual Erasure (CMRE) for Source-Bias-Robust Unsupervised E-Waste Clustering
SigLIP2, DINOv3, PE-Core, PCA, Spectral Clustering, SCMax

Proposed Cross-Model Residual Erasure (CMRE) for label-free clustering of 3,792 e-waste images in the BDC Satria Data 2026 semifinal, projecting out the dominant directions of SigLIP2 and Perception Encoder embeddings that DINOv3 cannot predict to remove photo-source bias without object labels or style proxies. Combined with SCMax cluster-count selection and spectral clustering, CMRE raised cross-source consistency from 0.524 to 0.975 and reached AMI 0.917 on 2,329 labelled images, documented in a scientific paper.
